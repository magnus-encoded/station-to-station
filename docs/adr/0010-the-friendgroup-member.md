# ADR-0010: The Friendgroup Member

**Status:** accepted (2026-08-11)

## Context

Social. The multi-timeline view is a symbol of her friendships with fellow concertgoers rather than a
data feature. She wants friends at future gigs and to find each other on arrival, and keepsakes read
to her as communal treasures rather than personal ones.

## Decision

**The Friendgroup Member is a first-class persona, and the whole meeting grammar exists for her**:
**Lanes**, **Crossings**, meeting green, the **Exchange**, and **Reconcile**.

**Communal does not mean shared state.** Her sense that a keepsake belongs to the group is honoured
as a social *act* — converting a night to a playlist offers to send it to whoever was there — with
each person owning their own copy afterwards. There is no canonical shared object, which is what
lets her be fully served under ADR-0003.

## Consequences

- **She is the strongest pull toward easy sharing**, and therefore the persona most often in tension
  with the Volunteer (ADR-0014), because communal keepsakes are crowd photographs.
- **Contacts are added in person**, which serves her directly (meeting is the point) while bounding
  exposure as a side effect.
- **Her features are best-effort by construction** (ADR-0004). A friend's media may never arrive and
  no screen of hers may break when it does not.
- Green means a night we shared, so it must never appear where there is no "we" (#145).

## Related
