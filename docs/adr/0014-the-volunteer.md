# ADR-0014: The Volunteer

**Status:** accepted as **not a user** (2026-08-11). The stakeholder the design protects.

## Context

Found by a persona pass over the media sharing specs, and the gap was stark: **15 of 15 user stories
on the contact's-eye view (#145) fitted no persona**, along with half of those on sharing (#144).
The un-personaed stories all had the same subject, the person in the frame who never installed this.

The young person working a festival for a wristband and little or no money: the gate, the bar, the
waste run. A recognisable role, and concrete in a way "a bystander" is not. *Would this harm the
volunteer at the bar?* has an answer. *Is this a privacy problem?* does not.

## Decision

**The Volunteer is a non-user stakeholder whose interest is represented deliberately, because
nothing else represents it.**

They cannot step out of your photograph, being at work in a spot somebody else assigned. They are
captured repeatedly and identifiably across several days at a known place and time. They are young,
and this record is built in decades.

**They stand for a class rather than only themselves.** The performer is explicitly excluded, since
performing is a deliberate public act and documenting setlists is setlist.fm's founding premise. A
**Contact** is excluded too, having an account and a face-to-face act of consent.

**They are owed something rather than merely not owed harm**, because every other persona's night
depends on their labour.

## Consequences

- **Sharing is granular by night and a global toggle is rejected.** Bulk removes the look, and the
  look is the judgement.
- **This app is a sharper risk than a camera roll.** Precision is the product, so a crowd shot here
  is bound to a named artist, a dated night and a located venue, which makes it an attendance record
  about a stranger.
- **A corollary that does not exist yet:** nothing distinguishes a crowd shot from a stage shot,
  which is the highest-signal distinction for this stakeholder.
- **Against the Journalist there is no tidy rule** (ADR-0012), and sometimes she wins.
- **Against the Organizer** (ADR-0013), the Volunteer wins.
