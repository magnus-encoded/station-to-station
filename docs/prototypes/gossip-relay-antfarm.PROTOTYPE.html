Gossip relay ant farm

Question: does a check-in actually cross a crowd under the rule the storm-gate enforces — bytes only ever cross a Contact edge — and would relaying through strangers who hold sealed bytes do materially better?

Both rules run on the same crowd and the same social graph, so the comparison is measured rather than argued.

Where the model comes from
  • Social Force Model (Helbing & Molnár 1995) — drive toward a goal, exponential repulsion from bodies and walls.
  • Crowd turbulence (Helbing, Johansson, Al-Abideen 2007) — above ~4–6 p/m² motion becomes random displacement rather than flow.
  • Mosh pits (Silverberg et al., Phys Rev Lett 2013) — a 2D gas of self-propelled particles; raising the alignment term gives the circle pit.
  • Opportunistic/DTN forwarding — power-law inter-contact times (Chaintreau et al.); social-based routing (BUBBLE Rap) where a few high-centrality bridges carry the traffic.
  • Heavy-tailed degree — real social graphs are not regular, so the median user and the mean user are different people. Toggle it below.
  • Citations are from memory and worth verifying before anyone quotes a number.
